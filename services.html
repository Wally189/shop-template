Available work

Independent final challenge before submission.

Tender Review

Useful when you have done the substantive drafting, the deadline still leaves time to make corrections, and you want somebody outside the writing process to test the response against the tender material.

The review challenges requirements and evaluation context, answer-to-question fit, material evidence and claims, ambiguity or omissions, then prioritises what deserves attention before submission.

The handover is a concise, structured review designed to support your final judgement and corrections.
