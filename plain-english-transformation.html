What we do / Traceable Plain-English & Complexity Transformation

Release candidate · make complexity usable

Make difficult information easier to use without quietly changing what it means.

A bounded transformation service for policies, procedures, technical instructions, decision material or other complex sources where ordinary rewriting is not enough. The result is designed around the user’s task while retaining a visible trail back to authoritative source meaning.

Contained candidate
This service is still evidence- and competence-gated. Subject matter that crosses legal, medical, financial or other specialist interpretation boundaries requires appropriate authority or is excluded.
The problem

Plain English is useful. Wrong English is not.

Dense source material can be technically accurate yet practically unusable. But simply shortening it can remove qualifications, conditions or decision logic that mattered.

The candidate therefore combines simplification with traceability: what the user needs to do, what the source actually requires, what has been reorganised, and where uncertainty still needs an accountable subject owner.

Possible outputs

  • Plain-English guided version of a bounded source.
  • Decision aid, checklist or step-by-step user journey.
  • Source-to-output traceability map for material requirements.
  • Ambiguity, contradiction and missing-owner flags.
  • Comprehension or task-oriented test questions where useful.

Boundaries

  • Meaning is not invented where the authoritative source is silent or unclear.
  • Specialist interpretation outside House competence is referred or excluded.
  • No cosmetic rewriting presented as evidence of improved comprehension.
  • Copyright, confidentiality and personal-data constraints are checked before source material is accepted.
  • Final accountable approval remains with the appropriate content owner where required.
What makes it different

Show your working.

Where practical, the transformed output should map material instructions or claims back to their source so the customer can see what was simplified, what was preserved and what still needs judgement. The commercial question remains whether buyers value that traceability enough to pay for it; the release candidate does not pretend that is already proved.

First step

Start with the task and the authority.

For contained review, describe what the source is, who must use it, what they need to achieve and who owns its meaning. Do not send confidential source material until a secure route is approved.
