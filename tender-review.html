The job

A final challenge, not a rewrite in disguise.

You have done the hard work. The draft is substantially complete. The risk now is familiarity: after repeated drafting, important omissions, unsupported claims and weak answer-to-question fit become difficult to see.

House of Carol reviews the submission independently against the material you provide and returns a prioritised set of findings for your final decision.

We look for

  • coverage of stated requirements and evaluation criteria;
  • claims that need stronger evidence or qualification;
  • answers that drift away from the question being asked;
  • material inconsistency, ambiguity or avoidable compliance concern;
  • important corrections that deserve attention before lower-value polishing.

Boundaries

What this service is not.

No guarantee of winning. Tender outcomes depend on the authority, competitors, evaluation and facts outside House of Carol's control.

No invented evidence. If a claim cannot be supported, the answer is not to make it sound more convincing.

No legal or procurement representation. The service is an independent commercial review, not legal advice or submission on your behalf.

No unlimited bid-writing. Materially different drafting or rewriting is separately scoped or declined.

Questions buyers usually ask

Before you enquire.

Do you write the tender for us?

No. This service is designed around independent review of a substantially complete submission. If the real need is full bid-writing, the fit is wrong.

Can you guarantee a better score or an award?

No. We can challenge the submission and the evidence it contains; we cannot control the buyer's evaluation or competitors.

What should I send in the first enquiry?

Only enough information to establish fit: the organisation, tender/reference, deadline, approximate size, draft status and what you most want challenged. Do not send confidential tender documents until the handling route and engagement have been confirmed.

What happens if you think the job cannot be done properly in time?

House of Carol should say so. A rushed review that cannot meet the agreed standard is not improved by accepting the work anyway.
