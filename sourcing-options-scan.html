Useful when

You know what you need, but not which claims deserve your attention.

The market may contain dozens of plausible products or suppliers, each described in its own language. The scan creates a comparison around your actual decision rather than reproducing everybody's marketing copy.

What you receive

  • Agreed comparison criteria.
  • Evidence-based shortlist of plausible options.
  • Structured comparison against the criteria.
  • Material limitations, unknowns and trade-offs.
  • Source links and decision factors or recommendation where supportable.

Boundaries

  • No undisclosed affiliate or commission bias.
  • No guarantee of a supplier's future performance.
  • No contractual commitment made for the customer.
  • No regulated procurement or legal advice where specialist status is required.
  • Where evidence is thin, the comparison says so.
First step

Start with the decision criteria, not the brand names.

Tell us what you are choosing, the must-have requirements, budget or constraints where relevant, and what would make an option unacceptable. That gives the research a finish line.
