The problem

The information is there. The usable structure is not.

A spreadsheet may need rebuilding from repeated PDFs, a table may be trapped in inconsistent documents, or a set of records may need cleaning before anybody can analyse it properly.

The useful result is not merely copied text. It is a structured dataset with uncertainty made visible and a clear relationship back to the source where practical.

What you receive

  • Agreed CSV, XLSX or structured table output.
  • Source-to-output traceability where proportionate.
  • Exception or uncertainty log for unclear material.
  • Validation summary and handover notes.
  • No silent filling of gaps where the source does not support an answer.

Boundaries

  • No unlawful scraping or unauthorised data handling.
  • No promise that poor scans or handwritten material can always be read reliably.
  • No regulated professional interpretation hidden inside data entry.
  • No high-risk automated decision making.
  • Sensitive or personal data requires an agreed handling route before transfer.
Quote basis

The source material determines the work.

For a firm quote, we need the approximate number of documents or pages, how consistent they are, the fields you need and the output format. Where the source varies materially, a safe sample inspection comes before the quote.
