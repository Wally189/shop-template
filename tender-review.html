When this is useful

The draft exists. The deadline is real. Familiarity is now the risk.

You and your colleagues have read the submission repeatedly. That is precisely when omissions become harder to spot. Tender Review adds a fresh reader whose job is not to admire the prose but to test whether the response earns its claims and answers the material requirement.

It works best when the submission is substantially complete and you still have enough time to make your own final corrections.

Start safely.

The first enquiry is for fit, deadline and scope. Do not send confidential tender content, credentials or sensitive evidence before the handling route is agreed.

  • claims that need stronger evidence, qualification or removal;

  • the order in which findings deserve attention before submission.

The scope in plain English

A second pair of eyes, not a second bidder.

The boundary is part of the product. It protects the quality of the review and makes it easier to decide whether House of Carol is genuinely useful for this job.

Inside Tender Review

  • requirements and evaluation mapping from the supplied tender material;
  • challenge of evidence, claims and answer-to-question fit;
  • identification of material ambiguity and obvious avoidable compliance concerns;
  • prioritised corrections and questions;
  • a concise written handover against the agreed submission version.

Outside Tender Review

  • guaranteeing a score, award or win;
  • inventing or embellishing evidence;
  • legal advice or procurement representation;
  • submitting through your procurement portal;
  • unlimited rewriting or full bid authorship hidden inside the review;
  • work that cannot be completed properly within the available time.

60-second fit check

Four questions before you spend time enquiring.

This check runs only in your browser. It sends and stores nothing.

Is the submission substantially complete?
Is there enough time left for a review and your own corrections?
Can you provide the tender requirements and the evidence behind material claims?
Is there a named person who will decide what changes before submission?

Useful to know

Questions worth answering before the deadline is close.

Do you write the tender for us?

No. The service is designed around independent review of a substantially complete submission. If the real need is full bid-writing, this is the wrong service.

Can you guarantee a better score or an award?

No. House of Carol can challenge the submission and the evidence it contains; the buyer's evaluation and competitors remain outside the service.

What belongs in the first enquiry?

Your organisation, tender title or reference, deadline, approximate submission size, draft status and the aspect you most want challenged. Keep confidential tender documents, credentials and sensitive evidence out of the first message.

What if there is not enough time to do a proper review?

We should say so. Accepting work that cannot be reviewed to the agreed standard would create activity, not value.

What if the tender changes after scope is agreed?

A material change is surfaced and re-scoped rather than silently absorbed. The review should always identify the exact version it challenged.
