Complaints & remedy

If something is wrong, make it specific and make it fixable.

A complaint should not disappear into a generic inbox. We separate the agreed service, what was delivered and the remedy being sought.

How the route works

  1. Acknowledge. We record the issue and the customer/job reference.
  2. Establish the facts. What was agreed? What was delivered? What is said to be wrong?
  3. Decide the route. Remedy a defect, request information needed to decide fairly, or explain why the matter sits outside the agreed service.
  4. Close clearly. Record the outcome and any learning needed to prevent recurrence.

What to include

Your name and organisation, the engagement/reference, what happened, the impact and what you would regard as a fair resolution.

Rights remain rights

Using this internal complaint route does not remove any rights or remedies either party has under the engagement or applicable law.
