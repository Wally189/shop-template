Accessibility

A website people can actually use.

House of Carol uses WCAG 2.2 AA as its internal accessibility baseline for this digital service. That is a design and testing standard, not a claim of external certification.

What we are building for

  • keyboard operation and visible focus;
  • logical headings and descriptive links;
  • high-contrast text and no colour-only meaning;
  • usable reflow down to 320 CSS pixels;
  • 200% text enlargement without loss of content or action;
  • reduced-motion preference;
  • plain language and manageable line lengths.

If something does not work

Once the business mailbox is live, accessibility problems can be reported through the same customer contact route. We will ask what you were trying to do, what prevented it and what reasonable alternative or correction is needed.

Current status

This is a contained preview. Automated and browser checks form part of the release gate; final human/owner review remains required before commercial release.
