Privacy

Collect less. Explain clearly.

This notice describes the contained preview as it actually operates now. It will be updated before any new customer-data processing is activated.

Current preview: there is no live enquiry submission, advertising tracker or analytics service on this site. The fit checker and enquiry brief builder operate in your browser and do not intentionally send their contents to House of Carol.

Who is responsible?

The operator is Alan W Gallagher trading as House of Carol, Bristol, United Kingdom.

Information processed by the website

The current site is a static GitHub Pages preview. Hosting infrastructure may process ordinary technical request information such as IP address, browser details and request logs under the hosting provider's own service operation. House of Carol does not currently use that information for customer profiling or advertising.

The browser-only fit checker and enquiry builder do not intentionally submit or persist what you type. Do not enter confidential tender material, credentials, sensitive personal data or other information that is unnecessary for preparing an initial enquiry.

Cookies and tracking

House of Carol has not intentionally deployed non-essential cookies, advertising pixels or analytics on this preview. If that changes, the technologies, purposes and any consent requirements will be assessed and explained before deployment.

When commercial enquiries open

Before a live email, form, document-upload route or other customer-data processing is activated, House of Carol will define the purpose, lawful basis where applicable, data required, retention period, recipients/processors, security controls and relevant rights. This notice will then be updated to match the real process.

Your rights and questions

Once a verified business privacy/contact route is active it will be published here. UK data-protection rights and the route to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office will be explained in the production notice appropriate to the processing then in use.

Changes

Material privacy changes should be made before or alongside the processing they describe, not after collection begins.
