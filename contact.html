A useful first message

Four things are normally enough.

01

The job: the service or business problem.

02

The scope: approximate pages, documents, journeys, questions, options or process size — whichever makes sense.

03

The result: what you need back or what decision the work needs to support.

04

The timing: any genuine deadline or decision date.

Please do not send passwords, portal credentials, confidential tender files, unnecessary personal information or sensitive customer/account data in the first general enquiry.

If the work needs more sensitive material, an appropriate route should be agreed first.

What happens next

A written quote turns the conversation into a defined job.

Where there is a fit, the quote confirms scope, deliverables, exclusions, information needed, proposed timing, price and what would count as a change. You retain the choice to accept it.
