Who is responsible

The website is operated by Alan W Gallagher trading as House of Carol, Bristol, United Kingdom. Before customer release, the verified business email and serviceable correspondence address will appear here and in the contact information.

What the website collects

House of Carol does not intentionally collect personal information through this contained preview. The interactive enquiry builder works in your browser and does not send its contents to House of Carol or a third-party form provider.

This preview is hosted using GitHub Pages. Like most hosting services, the host may process technical information needed to deliver and secure the service, such as IP address, browser/request information and logs, under its own service and privacy arrangements.

Cookies and analytics

House of Carol does not set analytics, advertising or preference cookies on this preview. We therefore do not operate a consent banner merely for decoration. If non-essential cookies or analytics are introduced later, the site and notice must be changed before they are used.

If you choose to email House of Carol, we will process the information you deliberately send so we can respond, assess fit, prepare a proposal or deliver an agreed service. The final notice will name the verified mailbox provider, purposes, lawful bases, retention approach, recipients/processors, any relevant transfers and your data-protection rights before customer release.

Your rights and complaints

UK data-protection law gives individuals rights that depend on the circumstances and lawful basis. The final customer notice will explain how to exercise them and how to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.
