About

House of Carol, Products & Services.

The customer-facing part of an independent British venture business, operated by Alan W Gallagher in Bristol.

The proposition

Small enough to stay accountable. Built to become much more.

House of Carol develops products, services and ventures around real problems. This website is the commercial shopfront for customer-facing products and services that have earned their place through defined scope and evidence.

Products & Services is a descriptive customer-facing part of House of Carol, not a separate legal entity.

Who is accountable?

Alan W Gallagher
Founder & Proprietor

Customers should know who stands behind the work. House of Carol may use different professional capabilities internally, but accountability is not passed around an org chart.

Commercial and legal documents identify the actual operator as required: Alan W Gallagher trading as House of Carol.

BRITISH RESTRAINT

Professional, not theatrical.

Clear language, measured claims and no need to sound enormous in order to take the work seriously.

SERVICE

The useful result comes first.

A product or service earns its place by solving a real job and leaving the customer with something they can use.

STEWARDSHIP

Treat information and trust carefully.

Scope, customer information, commitments and corrections are handled as responsibilities rather than sales collateral.

The wider House

Products & Services is not the corporate homepage.

The separate House of Carol corporate website exists for parent-company provenance and the wider venture portfolio. This site exists to help a customer understand and buy current products and services.
