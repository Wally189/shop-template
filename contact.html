A useful first message

Four things are normally enough.

01

The job: the service or business problem.

02

The scope: approximate pages, questions, users, process size or other useful boundary.

03

The result: what you need back or what decision the work should support.

04

The timing: any genuine deadline or decision date.

Do not send passwords, credentials, confidential tender files, unnecessary personal information or sensitive customer/account data in the first general enquiry.

If the work later needs sensitive material, an appropriate secure route must be agreed first.
