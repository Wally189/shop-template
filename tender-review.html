Tender Decision & Submission Review

Tender Review

Independent challenge before an important submission goes in.

When a tender is nearly finished, familiarity can make weaknesses harder to see. Tender Review gives a substantially complete response a fresh reading against the tender material you provide, with the remaining issues organised around what appears to matter most before submission.

This is most useful when the draft already exists.

The substantive response is already written. There is still enough time for a review and for you to make your own corrections. The tender documents and supporting evidence are available. You want somebody to challenge the response, not take over the bid.

If the real need is to write the submission from scratch, obtain legal advice or hand over the final procurement decision, this is not the right service.

What the review looks at

01

Did the response answer what was actually asked?

A polished answer can still miss the question. The review checks the relationship between material requirements or evaluation criteria and the response supplied.

02

Can the important claims carry their own weight?

Where a statement appears to need stronger evidence, qualification or correction, that should be visible before the bid is submitted rather than discovered afterwards.

03

What may be missing, ambiguous or need attention?

The review surfaces material gaps, inconsistencies and avoidable uncertainty at the strength the available material supports.

04

What deserves the remaining time?

Not every weakness matters equally. Findings are prioritised so the final editing effort can be directed deliberately rather than spent polishing everything again.

What you receive

  • A structured view of the material requirements or evaluation points against the submission, in the format appropriate to the agreed review.
  • Findings on evidence, claims, question fit, material gaps and ambiguity.
  • A prioritised list of corrections, questions or points requiring your decision.
  • A concise handover intended to help you make the final changes and submission decision.

Exact document format and scope are agreed before work begins rather than implied by this website.

A review, not a promise of winning.

Tender Review does not guarantee a score, shortlist, contract or win. It does not invent evidence or make unsupported claims stronger by wording alone. It is not legal advice or procurement representation, it does not submit through the procurement portal for you, and it is not unlimited rewriting or covert full bid authorship.

How it works

First, establish fit.

We check the deadline, state of the draft and kind of challenge required before confidential tender material is requested.

Then agree the boundary.

The documents to be reviewed, timing, expected handover and any material handling requirements are made clear before the engagement begins.

Then the review.

The agreed material is challenged against the scope, with findings recorded at the strength the evidence supports.

Then the handover.

You receive the agreed review and remain responsible for the final content, judgement and submission.

First contact and confidential material

For the first enquiry, do not send the tender itself, passwords, portal credentials or unnecessary confidential material. A useful first message normally needs only enough to establish fit: the submission deadline, whether the draft is substantially complete and what you most want challenged.

If the work progresses, the appropriate information-handling route can then be agreed before documents are exchanged.

Fit / no-fit

This looks closer to a fit if:

  • the response is substantially complete;
  • there is enough time for review and your corrections;
  • the tender material needed to understand the ask is available;
  • you retain the final decision and submission responsibility.

It is probably not a fit if:

  • you need the bid written from the beginning;
  • there is no realistic time to review and act on findings;
  • the source material is unavailable;
  • you need legal or regulatory advice or procurement representation;
  • you want a guaranteed result.

Price and quotation

No public price is approved at this stage. The eventual customer-facing route will state the real approved price or quoting basis plainly once it has been authorised. No placeholder number or invented “from” price is shown here.

Questions worth answering before the deadline is close

Will you rewrite my bid?

The service is a review of a substantially complete submission. Small wording examples may be useful to explain a finding, but the service does not quietly become unlimited rewriting or full authorship.

Can you guarantee a better score or a win?

No. The purpose is to provide an independent challenge and clearer findings before submission. The evaluation and award decision remain with the buyer or contracting organisation and no outcome is guaranteed.

How late is too late?

There must be enough time not only to perform the agreed review but for you to understand and act on the findings. The exact deadline is therefore part of the fit check rather than something the website should guess.

What do you need from me?

After fit and handling arrangements are agreed, the review will normally require the relevant tender documents, the substantially complete response and the evidence or source material necessary to test material claims. Exact requirements depend on scope.

Who makes the final submission decision?

You do. Tender Review supports the decision; it does not replace your judgement or responsibility for the final submission.
