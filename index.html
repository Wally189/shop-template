Recognise the moment?

The draft is done. Your judgement is tired.

You know the bid too well. The deadline is close enough to matter. Another round of general polishing will not tell you whether every material requirement is actually answered, whether the evidence carries the claim, or which weaknesses deserve the remaining time.

That is the moment Tender Review is designed for: not a rewrite, not a promise of winning, but a fresh challenge of a substantially complete submission.

01 · COVERAGE

Did the response answer the requirement?

We map the material asks and evaluation logic visible in the tender against the submission.

02 · EVIDENCE

Can the claims carry their own weight?

Unsupported, vague or over-confident claims are surfaced for strengthening, qualification or removal.

03 · PRIORITY

What deserves the final hour?

Findings are ordered so important corrections come before cosmetic polishing.

What you receive

A review you can act on.

Not a decorative report and not a cloud of consultancy language. The handover is built around decisions: what appears covered, what is weak or unsupported, what may be missing, and what deserves attention first.
