How we work

Clear before clever.

A professional service should reduce uncertainty, not wrap it in impressive language.

The engagement method

Four steps. One coherent piece of work.

01

Fit

Understand the actual job, deadline, information available and whether House of Carol is competent to help.

02

Scope

Define what is included, excluded, needed and what a finished job looks like before substantive work begins.

03

Challenge

Work from the agreed evidence, test the important assumptions and keep uncertainty visible.

04

Handover

Return a usable result, explain material limitations and leave the customer with a clear next action.

Operating principles

Good service has visible edges.

Define before beginning

Urgency does not make an unclear scope safer.

Separate fact from judgement

A conclusion should be as strong as the evidence behind it, not stronger.

Use the least information necessary

We do not ask for sensitive or confidential material merely because it might be useful later.

Keep QA separate from acceptance

Internal checking tests whether the work meets its specification. The customer decides whether the delivered result is accepted for the agreed purpose.

Correct defects

When something is wrong, the professional response is to identify it, fix it and learn from it.

One coherent customer experience

Customers should not have to understand internal functions in order to get a clear answer or a finished result.

Scope is not paperwork around the service. Scope is part of the service.

When we say no

A wrong fit is still a wrong fit.

House of Carol should decline, narrow or route work when the task requires competence it does not hold, the evidence needed to do the job properly is unavailable, the deadline makes a credible result impossible, or the request crosses a legal, security or professional boundary.

A disclaimer is not a substitute for competence.
