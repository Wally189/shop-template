How we work

Clear before clever.

  1. 1
    Define the job.

    Agree the exact question, material and boundary before work starts.

  2. 2
    Work from evidence.

    Separate what is supported from assumptions, gaps and unresolved points.

  3. 3
    Keep the boundary visible.

    Do not manufacture certainty, competence or scope that is not there.

  4. 4
    Hand back something usable.

    Return a clear result that supports the customer’s next decision.

Fit

Designed for a defined commercial task.

These services are intended for small UK businesses that can provide the information needed to do the work properly and value a clear, bounded piece of support. They are not a substitute for regulated professional advice, an in-house legal team or unlimited outsourced administration.
