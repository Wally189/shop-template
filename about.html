About House of Carol

An independent business, built around useful work.

House of Carol is founded and operated by Alan W Gallagher in Bristol. It undertakes useful, defined pieces of work and develops products and services that earn their place by solving real problems.

The business

The customer-facing business is deliberately simpler than the machinery used to organise the work behind it.

What matters here is straightforward: understand the job, agree what is being done, work carefully, and return something useful. House of Carol does not need a fictional team, invented history or consultancy theatre to make that proposition sound larger than it is.

Where the business has evidence, it should show it. Where it does not yet have customer proof, it should earn it rather than manufacture the appearance of maturity.

Founder & proprietor

Alan W Gallagher

A business and technical operations professional with more than twenty years of experience across IT services, information-security administration, healthcare, customer operations, legal administration and regulated service environments.

Across that career, the recurring work has been practical rather than theatrical: keeping complicated activity moving; translating between technical teams, managers, suppliers and operational users; clarifying requirements; coordinating workflows; improving records and documentation; resolving service problems; and creating enough structure for people to make decisions and follow them through.

His experience includes service operations and technical liaison, access and information-security administration, hardware procurement and lifecycle coordination, customer administration, process improvement, reporting and governance support. He has also supported significant operational change, including Brexit-transition activity and pandemic remote-working deployment.

That breadth matters to House of Carol because many small-business problems do not arrive neatly labelled. They sit between a process, a document, a system, a customer expectation and a decision somebody still has to make.

A working instinct

A bit of mess to sort out?

Alan describes himself as useful where there are unclear processes, competing priorities, technical detail that needs translating, or work that needs a proper trail of actions, owners and decisions.

The House turns that instinct into a disciplined commercial approach: clarify the problem, separate fact from assumption, create the structure the job actually needs and keep moving towards a usable result.
