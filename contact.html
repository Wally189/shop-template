Bring us a problem

You do not need to know the name of the service before getting in touch.

Describe the job you are trying to get done, what is making it difficult and what a useful result would look like. House of Carol uses first contact to establish whether there is a sensible fit and what, if anything, should happen next.

A useful first message

Short, specific and safe.

A useful first message normally needs your name and email address, plus one substantive answer: what are you trying to get done?

Useful context, where you know it, includes a deadline or decision date, what already exists, and what would be different if the job were properly finished.

Do not send sensitive material in the first message.

Please do not send passwords, portal credentials, confidential tender files, unnecessary personal information or sensitive customer or account data. If the work needs more sensitive material, an appropriate route should be agreed first.
