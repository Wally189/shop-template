About House of Carol

Intelligence. Judgement. Impact.

Three words we use as a working test, not a piece of corporate wallpaper.

The business

An independent British portfolio venture business.

House of Carol is operated by Alan W Gallagher in Bristol. It exists to build useful, ethical businesses, products and services using human skill, disciplined processes, modern technology and evidence from the wider world.

Business Services is a descriptive customer-facing layer of House of Carol, not a separate legal entity.

INTELLIGENCE

Understand before acting.

Ask the better question, find the relevant evidence and resist the temptation to start with the answer you wanted.

JUDGEMENT

Decide what matters.

Evidence still needs proportion, ethics, taste, commercial sense and the willingness to say no.

IMPACT

Change something useful.

A piece of work should improve a real decision, result, experience, capability, cost, risk or understanding.

The feel we are after

Established behaviour, without invented history.

House of Carol is new. We do not fake longevity, customer numbers, awards or corporate scale.

What we can build from day one is the behaviour people associate with mature businesses: coherent documents, careful service boundaries, predictable communication, good typography, clear complaints and remedy routes and no visible improvisation.

Who you deal with

A named accountable proprietor.

Customer communications are issued by Alan W Gallagher, Founder & Proprietor. House of Carol may use specialised internal functions and technology behind the work, but customers should never have to navigate an invented org chart.
