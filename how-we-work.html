How we work

No theatre. No mystery box.

A mature service is easier to trust when you can see how decisions are made, what changes state and where the boundaries sit.

01 · UNDERSTAND

Start with the actual job.

What is the business problem, deadline, available evidence and result you need?

02 · CHECK

Test fit before selling.

Do we have the competence, information, capacity and lawful basis to do useful work?

03 · AGREE

Make the deal legible.

Scope, exclusions, dependencies, fee, timing and acceptance are clear before substantive work begins.

The decision rule

Progress is not the only respectable answer.

Depending on the facts, House of Carol can progress, ask for clarification, route you elsewhere, decline the work or stop if a legal, ethical, security or competence boundary is reached.

That is how a small business avoids pretending to be an expert in everything.

Technology & AI

Tools assist the work. They do not own the judgement.

House of Carol is AI-enabled, and we use appropriate digital tools for research, structure, checking and production. The important bit is the control around them: source discipline, verification, privacy, security and accountable human judgement.

We do not sell “AI magic”, and we do not treat a machine-generated sentence as evidence merely because it sounds certain.

If something goes wrong

Separate the defect from the drama.

We look at what was agreed, what was delivered and the specific remedy being requested. Material defects are corrected rather than hidden. If the issue sits outside the agreed service, we explain why.
