Contained release candidate

Price should follow a real boundary, not a decorative calculator.

The previous contained preview carried indicative package prices before the rebuilt whole-business project had authorised them as current commercial truth. Those figures are therefore not carried into this release candidate.

Release rule

Written quote first.

Before live release, each approved offer will have either a tested public price or a clear written-quote route. A quote must state scope, deliverables, exclusions, customer responsibilities, timing, price, payment method and what would require a variation.

Payment dependency
At least one customer payment method must be configured, tested and reconcilable before sales open. The contained candidate does not invent a live payment link or bank route.
