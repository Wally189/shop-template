Useful when

You need an answer, not a pile of search results.

The question might concern a market, supplier choice, policy change, operational decision or unfamiliar business topic. The job is to define the question tightly enough that the research can finish and the answer can support a real decision.

What you receive

  • Clear framing of the agreed question.
  • Source review and concise findings.
  • Known, inferred and uncertain points distinguished.
  • Options and implications where requested and supportable.
  • Source list and a decision-ready written brief.

What it is not

  • Not legal, financial or medical advice.
  • Not invented primary research.
  • Not an unbounded academic literature review.
  • Not certainty where the available evidence is mixed.
  • Not a substitute for a regulated specialist where one is required.
Start with the question

A good brief begins by making the decision explicit.

Tell us what you need to decide, what you already know, the deadline and how deep the answer needs to go. If the question is too broad to price responsibly, we will narrow it before quoting.
