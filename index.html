Recognise the moment?

The draft is done. Your judgement is tired.

You know the bid too well. The deadline is close enough to matter. Another round of general polishing will not tell you whether every material requirement is actually answered, whether the evidence carries the claim, or which weaknesses deserve the remaining time.

That is the moment Tender Review is designed for: not a rewrite, not a promise of winning, but a fresh challenge of a substantially complete submission.

A useful decision before an enquiry

Know whether this is the right service.

A professional service should make a useful no-fit decision as easy as a sale. Tender Review works for a narrow moment in the tender process; if the real need is different, the website should tell you before either side wastes time.

Looks like a fit

The submission is substantially complete; the deadline leaves time for review and your corrections; the tender documents and evidence are available; someone on your side owns the final decision.

Needs a conversation

The draft is close but one dependency is unclear — perhaps timing, available evidence or what you most want challenged. Prepare a short first-contact brief without confidential tender content.

Not this service

You need the tender written from scratch, legal or regulated advice, portal submission, invented evidence, or a guaranteed outcome. Tender Review is deliberately not those things.
