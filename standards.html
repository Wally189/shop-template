House standards

The standard is part of the product.

Polish matters. So do accessibility, privacy, security, truthful claims, commercial clarity and a credible route for putting things right.

ACCESSIBILITY

Designed in, not inspected on later

Public customer journeys target WCAG 2.2 AA quality, keyboard operation, responsive reflow, visible focus and clear semantic structure.

PRIVACY

Collect less

House of Carol starts from data minimisation. A new form, tracker or third-party service has to earn its place and be explained properly.

SECURITY

Proportionate controls

Access, configuration, deployment and customer information are treated as operating responsibilities, not merely technical details.

CLAIMS

Evidence before adjectives

No fabricated testimonials, fake scarcity, invented longevity, guaranteed outcomes or claims stronger than the evidence behind them.

COMMERCIAL

Know what you are agreeing to

Scope, exclusions, price or quoting basis, payment terms and material conditions should be clear before commitment.

CORRECTION

Put defects right

Errors and complaints are not hidden to preserve appearances. They are classified, corrected and used to improve the service.

Why this matters

Because polish without discipline is theatre.

House of Carol is building its trading history now. It will not manufacture age, scale or social proof to appear established.

The mature-business impression should instead come from the things customers can actually experience: coherent information, predictable service, clear boundaries, careful communication, accessible design and accountable decisions.

Customer recourse

The standards need an exit door.

If you find a factual error, accessibility barrier, service defect or complaint, House of Carol provides a route to report it and will distinguish the issue from the remedy rather than treating feedback as an inconvenience.
