THE JOB

Not to rewrite your bid.

To test whether it answers the requirement, supports its claims and gives the evaluator what they need to assess it.

Independent reviewDefined scopeEvidence-led findingsYour final decision

What you receive

A review organised around what you need to decide before submission.

The output is designed to help you spend the remaining time on the issues that matter most, rather than produce another long report for its own sake.

The review

  • Requirement mapping. What the tender asks for, where your response addresses it and where the connection is weak or missing.
  • Evaluation challenge. Whether the submission makes it easy to see the evidence relevant to the stated criteria and scoring logic.
  • Evidence and claim check. Unsupported, ambiguous or over-stated claims are separated from evidence you can actually stand behind.
  • Compliance concerns. Material response gaps, contradictions, unanswered instructions or obvious submission risks are surfaced for your attention.
  • Prioritised corrections. Findings are organised so you can distinguish important fixes from optional polishing.
  • Decision-ready handover. You receive a concise review and retain responsibility for the final bid and submission.

Good fit

You need a final challenge, not someone to invent the bid for you.

Usually a good fit

Your submission is substantially complete; the tender and evaluation information are available; your organisation owns the underlying facts and evidence; and you want an independent check before submission.

Likely to be declined or re-scoped

You need full bid authorship from a blank page, legal or procurement advice, evidence invented or embellished, portal submission, an outcome guarantee, or a review window too short to do responsible work.

What this service does not promise

A stronger decision, not a guaranteed result.

No reviewer can honestly promise that a tender will win. Award decisions depend on the competition, the authority, the evaluation and facts outside House of Carol's control.

The service therefore promises the defined review work and a usable handover—not a win rate, a score increase or certainty that the customer does not possess.
