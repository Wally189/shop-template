About House of Carol

An independent business, built around useful work.

House of Carol is founded and operated by Alan W Gallagher in Bristol. It undertakes useful, defined pieces of work and develops products and services that earn their place by solving real problems.

Alan W Gallagher

Founder & proprietor

A business and technical operations professional with more than twenty years of experience across IT services, information-security administration, healthcare, customer operations, legal administration and regulated service environments.

Across that career, the recurring work has been practical: keeping complicated activity moving; translating between technical teams, managers, suppliers and operational users; clarifying requirements; coordinating workflows; improving records and documentation; resolving service problems; and creating enough structure for people to make decisions and follow them through.

That breadth matters because many small-business problems do not arrive neatly labelled. They sit between a process, a document, a system, a customer expectation and a decision somebody still has to make.

A working instinct

Useful where there is a bit of mess to sort out.

Unclear processes, competing priorities, technical detail that needs translating, or work that needs a proper trail of actions, owners and decisions.

The House turns that instinct into a disciplined commercial approach: clarify the problem, separate fact from assumption, create the structure the job actually needs and keep moving towards a usable result.
