Tender Decision & Submission Review

Before you submit, find the gaps you no longer see.

A bounded independent review of a substantially complete tender—designed to test requirement coverage, evidence, compliance and scoring logic while there is still time to act.

Check whether it fitsPrepare an enquiry

The problem

You know the bid too well.

By the final stages of a tender, the drafting team can explain what every sentence was meant to say. Evaluators cannot. They see only the submission in front of them, against the requirements and scoring criteria they have been given.

This review creates deliberate distance. It asks whether the submission actually carries the evidence, clarity and answer-fit you think it does.

What we examine

  • Mandatory requirements and evaluation criteria
  • Whether answers address the question actually asked
  • Claims that need stronger evidence or qualification
  • Compliance, inconsistency and omission risks
  • Areas where clarity or prioritisation may affect scoring logic

Boundaries

Useful because it knows what it is not.

Inside the service

Independent review, evidence challenge, requirement mapping, prioritised corrections and a clear handover.

Outside the service

No guaranteed award, invented evidence, legal advice, procurement-portal submission or unlimited rewriting/full authorship hidden inside the review.

Questions customers usually have

Before you commit.

Good buying decisions happen when awkward details are visible early.

Can you tell me whether we will win?

No. A responsible reviewer can test the submission and explain weaknesses; nobody can honestly guarantee the evaluator’s decision.

Will you rewrite the whole bid?

Not under this service. The purpose is independent review. Materially different work must be separately scoped rather than quietly expanding the engagement.

Can you invent or strengthen evidence for us?

We can help you spot where evidence is weak or missing. We will not manufacture facts, case studies, credentials or results.

What if the deadline is extremely close?

Timing is part of the fit decision. We will not accept a deadline that makes a useful, controlled review unrealistic.

What do you need from us?

The current tender pack and amendments, evaluation criteria, substantially complete response, relevant supporting evidence, exact deadline and a named decision-maker.
