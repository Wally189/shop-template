How pricing works

No mystery day-rate arithmetic.

House of Carol prices bounded pieces of work. The important variables are the amount of material, the number of moving parts, the depth of checking and whether the deadline materially changes how the work has to be done.

The calculator works entirely in your browser. It does not transmit or store your selections. It asks only for scope information — not confidential documents, passwords or sensitive customer data.

Before work starts
A firm written quote sets out the exact scope, deliverables, exclusions, timing, price and what would require a change. An indicative website price is not permission for scope to drift.
