What we do / One Bounded Process Fix

Release candidate · simplify work

Fix one recurring process without buying a transformation programme.

Bring one business process that has become slow, duplicated, confusing or too dependent on workarounds. House of Carol maps what actually happens, identifies the avoidable friction and defines the smallest practical improvement.

Contained candidate
Exact public price and live order/payment route are not yet authorised. A firm written quote will set the boundary before any released engagement starts.
The situation

Everyone knows the workaround. Nobody is quite sure why it is necessary.

Processes often accumulate inbox steps, duplicated trackers, manual re-entry, unclear ownership and software bought for problems that were never properly defined.

The working rule is existing-tools-first. Automation or a new product is considered only when it is genuinely the smallest safe answer.

What the candidate is designed to return

  • A clear current-state workflow or equivalent representation.
  • Friction, waiting, duplication and failure points.
  • Ownership and decision gaps that materially affect the work.
  • A simplified future-state sequence.
  • A practical improvement list ordered by value, risk and effort.
  • Existing-tool or software-rationalisation opportunities where they genuinely help.

Boundaries

  • One bounded process unless separately quoted.
  • No organisation-wide transformation programme hidden inside the engagement.
  • No automatic software purchase, migration or subscription recommendation.
  • No employment, legal, tax or regulated-professional advice outside competence.
  • No savings claim until real baseline and outcome evidence exists.
Evidence of value

The before-state matters.

The useful test is not whether the future-state diagram looks elegant. Where feasible, the work should identify observable before-state measures—steps, waiting, duplication, errors, handoffs, tool burden or proprietor time—so later improvement can be judged against something real.

First step

Name the recurring job, not the tool you think you need.

For contained review, start with what triggers the process, what finished means, who is involved and where the friction appears. Do not send credentials or unnecessary sensitive information.
