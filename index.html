Tender Decision & Submission Review

Independent challenge for a substantially complete tender before you submit it.

Useful when the bid is largely written, the deadline is real and you need someone outside the drafting process to test whether the submission actually answers the requirement and supports its claims.

You receive

  • requirement and evaluation-criterion mapping;
  • evidence gaps and unsupported claims identified;
  • compliance and response weaknesses surfaced;
  • prioritised corrections and questions;
  • a concise review for your final decision.

Not bid-writing in disguise. No promise of winning, invented evidence, legal advice, procurement-portal submission or unlimited rewriting.

How we work

Professional support should make the next decision clearer.

01

Fit check

We establish the exact job, deadline, material available and whether House of Carol is genuinely the right fit.

02

Scope

We agree what is being reviewed, what you will receive, what is excluded and what information is needed.

03

Independent work

We challenge the material against the agreed requirements and keep fact, judgement, assumption and uncertainty distinct.

04

Handover

You receive prioritised findings you can use. The final commercial decision remains yours.

Is it for you?

A good fit has a defined job and enough evidence to do it properly.

Usually a good fit

A small UK business with a specific commercial task, a named decision-maker, usable source material and a preference for clear, bounded support.

Usually not a good fit

An open-ended request to “fix everything”, a need for regulated professional advice, a demand for guaranteed outcomes, or work that depends on inventing evidence or exceeding our competence.

Why House of Carol?

Careful is a feature, not a delay.

Small businesses often have important work without a specialist team sitting behind it. Our role is not to add consultancy theatre. It is to bring disciplined attention to a defined task, challenge what needs challenging and leave you with something useful.

We use technology and AI behind the scenes where they genuinely improve the work. They are tools, not the proposition, and they do not replace accountable judgement.
