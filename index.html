Tender Decision & Submission Review

Independent challenge for a substantially complete tender before you submit it.

For businesses that can do the work but want another careful mind on the submission: requirement coverage, evidence, compliance, question-fit and scoring logic.

You receive

  • requirement and evaluation mapping;
  • evidence gaps and unsupported claims surfaced;
  • compliance and response weaknesses identified;
  • prioritised corrections and questions;
  • a concise written handover.

No false certainty. We do not promise an award, invent evidence, give legal advice or turn a bounded review into unlimited bid-writing.
