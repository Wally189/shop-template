A useful first message

Short, specific and safe.

A useful first message normally includes your name, organisation where relevant, what you are trying to get done, any deadline or decision date, what already exists and what would be different if the job were properly finished.

Please do not send passwords, portal credentials, confidential tender files, unnecessary personal information or sensitive customer/account data in the first general enquiry.

If the work needs more sensitive material, an appropriate route should be agreed first.

If the enquiry is not a sensible House of Carol fit, we should say so rather than manufacture an engagement.
