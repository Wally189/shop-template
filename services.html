House of Carol undertakes defined pieces of business work where an independent view, careful analysis or focused delivery can make the next decision easier.

The rule

The public service list stays deliberately short. A service appears here when the House can define what it does, what it does not do and what the customer should receive.

Ideas in development are not dressed up as products. As real services earn their place, this index can grow without changing that rule.
